Privacy policy

This policy describes the privately operated Hermes Gmail integration, including its local Google Drive connection, and distinguishes that application from these public information pages.

1. Information the application can access

Only the Google account that authorizes the application is accessed, within the permissions granted. Gmail data can include messages, headers, attachments, labels, filters and authorized mailbox settings. Drive data can include file and folder names, identifiers, metadata, contents and sharing information.

Access supports requested email and file-management features. Changes such as sending a message, creating or moving a file, or changing sharing are performed as part of tasks requested by the account holder. Loading this website does not authorize Google access or perform these operations.

2. How information is used

Google data is used to carry out the account holder’s requested tasks, such as finding information, organizing email or files, preparing drafts and summaries, and saving requested outputs. The application does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or assemble datasets to train general-purpose AI models.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs are subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and local task state are stored on the operator’s computer. Files, email excerpts, summaries, conversation records and operational logs may be retained in locations selected or configured by the operator. There is no central application database behind this website and no fixed automatic deletion period for local task outputs.

Local credentials are protected by operating-system access controls. The operator is responsible for workstation security, backups and access to saved outputs. Connections to Google and configured cloud services use their HTTPS APIs.

4. Transfers and configured services

Google processes requests sent to its APIs. When the account holder requests AI-assisted work using a cloud model, selected content and task instructions may be sent to the AI provider configured in Hermes Agent. That provider’s processing and retention depend on its terms and the operator’s selected data controls; this policy does not claim that every third-party provider retains nothing.

Content may also be delivered to email recipients or uploaded or shared with people chosen by the account holder. These are requested task operations, not automatic publication. Google data is not intentionally posted to this public website or its source repository.

The operator should review the configured provider’s privacy and data controls before forwarding sensitive Google content. The integration itself does not use Google data to train a general-purpose model.

5. This public website

The site contains static application information and this policy. It has no sign-in, contact form, advertising, application analytics script or Google API connection. It does not receive OAuth credentials, email contents or Drive files.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary request information, including IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure it. See the GitHub privacy statement for the hosting provider’s practices.

6. Control, revocation and deletion

The account holder can revoke access through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents continued authorized API access but does not automatically erase files, summaries, logs or backups already saved locally or sent to a configured service or chosen recipient.

The operator can remove local credentials and saved outputs, delete or unshare files in Drive, and manage retention with any configured AI provider. Deletion of Google-account data is separate from deleting locally downloaded copies.

7. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the application’s data-handling practices change. Material new uses should be disclosed before they are enabled. Questions or deletion requests about the operator’s copy of the application can be sent to luolinshan123@gmail.com.
