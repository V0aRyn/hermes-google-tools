Application information

A local connection to Gmail and Google Drive.

Hermes Gmail is a privately operated integration used with Hermes Agent for personal email and file-management tasks.

What the application does

The application connects to Google APIs after the account holder authorizes access. Depending on the permissions granted and the tasks requested, it can search and organize email, read selected messages or attachments, prepare drafts, send requested messages, and manage authorized email settings.

The Google Drive connection can find, read, download and upload files, organize folders, and apply sharing changes requested by the account holder. It does not make files public simply by connecting.

How it operates

The integration runs on the operator’s computer. Google sign-in and consent happen through Google’s authorization pages; OAuth credentials are stored in local configuration, not on this website.

When the account holder asks for AI-assisted processing, selected content may be sent to the configured AI service. Requested email delivery and file sharing can also send content to the recipients chosen by the account holder. The privacy policy explains access, use, storage and these transfers.

These public pages provide information only. There is no sign-up form or Google sign-in on this website, and it does not receive email contents, Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Operator and support

This integration is operated privately by the account holder. It is not a Google product and is not an official service operated by Nous Research.
